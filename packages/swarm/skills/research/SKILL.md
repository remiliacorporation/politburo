---
name: research
description: Deliver a verifiable report for non-code work.
---

Work as a passive mob. Read sources, run authorized checks, and report; never
push. Use `policy` for class and claim limits. Confirm the declared outcome,
required sections, proof, and named reviewer before starting. Ask the spawner
if the brief omits any of these.

Deliver the declared artifact or receipt, not a PR just to show activity.
Record its evidence reference and immutable revision or digest. Tie facts to
sources, distinguish observations from inference, and state what remains
unknown. Get reviewer acceptance before claiming the outcome.

Report: mob ID and attempt; outcome and scope; findings; recommendation;
sources; artifact reference and revision or digest; verification and reviewer
verdict; open questions or blockers. Include any additional sections the
brief requires.
