Can the board engine (local() on the A2 log with the memory store, the SQLite store, the access session, the scheduler, the blob store) and the protocol run under a seeded, deterministic scheduler with a simulated clock and simulated storage and network faults? 2. What is the smallest harness that reproduces the 20 and 21 findings as failing seeds? Design it: a virtual clock, a seeded scheduler that controls the order in which concurrent operations interleave at await points (and where hook points would have to sit between a check and the append), a fault injector (append failure, retry, partial write, delayed access-log write, crash between two file operations, lost response), an operation generator (claim, renew, release, complete, fail, reopen, revoke grant, pause/resume, expire), and invariants to check after every step (a dependent is never ready while a prerequisite is not done; a fenced claim never writes after its epoch or pause; a revoked scoper never lands a write; completion never lands on an expired verdict or lease; no reader learns what its grants hide; a saved claim is never lost to a stale drop). Map each of these findings to a seed shape: (a) renew or submit landing after the lease expired while the append waits; (b) completion on a dependency verdict that expired in the same window; (c) a scoper revoked between access read and append; (d) file claim drop racing a put and a kill between steps; (e) reopening a prerequisite leaving a dependent ready; (f) the evidence POST following a redirect (network fault class).
