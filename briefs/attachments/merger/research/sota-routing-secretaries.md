# Replace the secretary session, not the secretary role

## Executive summary and ranked recommendations

1. **Ship a model-free secretary first.** File delivery, deadlines, digests, notifications, and nearly all job mechanics are explicit policy. The live failure to fix is missed wakes, not poor semantic routing. Move supervision out of chat sessions into a pinned, launchd-managed service in `packages/swarm`. Keep the file bridge and follow the merger freeze and cutover order. Get bonklek's approval before retiring the Haiku sessions: their model is a standing ruling, and the pilot is not a policy override.
2. **Use TypeSafe's typed Jev classifier, not `typesafe/jev-router`, for ambiguous mail and intake.** The exact slug in the question is a hosted *model selector*: it chooses a model and thinking effort and returns that model's generated answer. The separate `typesafe/jev-1.14` Decisions model answers a typed question like “which of these three authorized queues should receive this?” Neither should be needed to deliver normal mail.
3. **Run Terra as a fixed, stateless advisor.** Give it one short, revisioned case packet, no tools, and a strict answer schema. Keep memory on the local board, not in a live prompt. Send cases that need an explanation, reconciliation, or a plan; not failed notifications, quarantined input, or routine retries. Use `gpt-5.6-terra` as the requested benchmark. Also test the newer `gpt-6.1-terra`: it has lower published prices. Do not make either model the authority to push, merge, retire, grant access, or answer for the owner.
4. **Pilot semantic intake before changing sprite selection.** It is a better first classifier task than mail that already carries a valid route. Later, test per-task model and thinking recommendations against the existing `fallback/codex-cursor` ladder. Keep quota/cooldown failover deterministic. Delay per-turn hosted routing until local results justify the added exposure and cost.

Research checked 2026-11-22 UTC. Live findings below refer to the dated merger snapshot, not a new production probe. No model was run. All latency and spend estimates that follow are either attributed or explicitly hypothetical.

## 3. What current practice says

**Model routing and cascades.** Routing predicts which model to use *before* generation. A cascade spends more only after a first decision or answer is insufficient. Availability fallback is different: it reacts to service errors, not judgment. Tigeryard needs all three concepts kept separate.

- [RouteLLM (paper revised 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.18665) learns strong-vs-cheap routing from preference data. Its [author write-up](https://www.lmsys.org/blog/2024-07-01-routellm/) reports over 85%, 45%, and 35% cost reductions on MT-Bench, MMLU and GSM8K at 95% of GPT-4 performance. Thresholds and transfer differ by workload. [FrugalGPT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.05176) is the earlier cascade reference (up to 98% cost reduction on selected tasks, not a typical rate). [A Unified Approach, revised October 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10347) combines routing and cascades and emphasizes quality estimation. [Beyond GPT-5, DAI 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12631) reports matching its strongest single-model average at 27% lower cost or about 90% of performance at 63% lower cost. These are benchmark costs, not secretary token figures.
- [OpenRouter Auto](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/routing/routers/auto-router.md) rates eleven aspects of a request, then weighs live price, cache, eligibility and benchmark performance. [Auto Code](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/routing/routers/auto-code.md) tunes the same engine for coding, favors effort changes over model switches to preserve cache, and documents hard plugin model allowlists, unlike the `typesafe/jev-router` include preferences. Its [router benchmark](https://openrouter.ai/benchmarks/routers) reports cost and score by task suite, not generic savings. OpenRouter's [Jev-verified RAG cascade](https://openrouter.ai/docs/cookbook/evaluate-and-optimize/jev-verified-cascade.md) reports $0.012 vs $0.175 for 50 questions, about 7% of Astra-only cost. Which baseline sent a wrong answer differed across two judges; the cascade sent none in either run. Small worked example, not an SLA.
- [Not Diamond Code, August 2026](https://www.notdiamond.ai/blog/not-diamond-code-intelligent-model-routing-for-coding-agents) selects models and reasoning per agent turn. Vendor reports 39–61% lower inference costs at roughly Opus 4.8 xhigh benchmark quality, and over 20% lower spend in early access; its [September methodology](https://www.notdiamond.ai/blog/interactive-benchmarks-a-new-methodology-for-evaluating-model-routing) argues for end-to-end interactive tasks, not fixed prompt replay alone. [Legacy API docs](https://docs.notdiamond.ai/docs/what-is-not-diamond) announce a December 16, 2026 retirement. [Martian RouterBench](https://withmartian.com/post/introducing-routerbench) studies predictive routing; [Martian's 2025 production write-up](https://withmartian.com/post/up-and-to-the-left) reports a help-chat deployment with 52.4% lower error rate and 92% lower cost. Vendor-reported, workload-specific. Neither vendor is needed to run the proposed secretary.

**Code owns the workflow.** Anthropic's [Building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents) distinguishes predefined workflows from agents and recommends adding autonomy only when needed. [OpenAI Agents SDK orchestration](https://openai.github.io/openai-agents-python/multi_agent/) distinguishes code routing from model delegation. [LangGraph's 2026 Jev write-up](https://www.langchain.com/blog/building-prod-with-jev-and-langgraph) puts bounded semantic questions in durable graph nodes and leaves retries/state to the runtime. [Akka's September 2026 Jev demo](https://akka.io/blog/fast-cheap-agent-decisions) shows the same split with code acting on structured decisions; its performance comparisons cite TypeSafe's evals rather than independent office benchmarks. [Temporal's AI cookbook](https://docs.temporal.io/ai/cookbook) is another durable-workflow reference, not a reason to add an external orchestrator. Locally, the Python worker and board already cover the necessary shape.

**Mail, supervision and context savings.** [Gas Town](https://github.com/steveyegge/gastown) persists work, has built-in mail, and uses a Go daemon for heartbeat supervision with a higher-level AI triage role. Use that separation, not its whole role tree. [MCP Agent Mail](https://github.com/Dicklesworthstone/mcp_agent_mail) offers repo-scoped identities, threaded inbox/outbox, explicit acknowledgements and advisory reservations backed by auditable artifacts. Borrow the acknowledgement and reservation semantics, not a second mail store. [Claude Code agent teams](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-teams.md) use a validated JSON mailbox per teammate and a shared dependency-aware task list; a send is successful only after the mailbox write. That does not solve cloud-to-Mac delivery or the office's multi-repo grants. [Anthropic's 2025 production research system](https://www.anthropic.com/research/multi-agent-research-system) reports about 15× the tokens of chat for multi-agent research, a warning about always-on model supervisors. [Advanced tool use](https://www.anthropic.com/research/advanced-tool-use) reports 43,588 → 27,297 average tokens (37%) with programmatic tool calling on its complex research tasks; [code execution with MCP](https://www.anthropic.com/research/code-execution-with-mcp) illustrates 150K → 2K (98.7%) by narrowing what's put in context. The latter is a worked example, not a fleet baseline. None of the mail systems provided a verified Haiku-replacement token benchmark.

The duty list is broader than those tools:

- **D/C, relay and observation:** Watch PRs/branches/CI and consume mob reports; turn *typed* event states into headers, rearm without the two-hour chat dependency, register the out-queue on startup, emit one-line previews and the structured header. The mailbox and digest code already does delivery. Git/CI polling, alerting and announcing the service need adapters. Mob cloud results still use final messages, PRs and spawner-generated file mail; do not assume automated remote board reporting is live. An unstructured mob handoff with no declared route is **J** to interpret; the spawner or an explicit intake path handles it, not the transport. Administrative `sec.log` is counted/logged, not sent for model analysis.
- **D/C, core support:** Hildy also delivers core out-mail, manages core wakes, keeps `core-watch.sh`/keep-awake alive and watches for owner items. Preserve PR-rate, stall and stuck-mail checks, PR log, manual/core handled records, and broadcast expansion from a registered tag list. Replace temporary `core_mail.sh` with a pinned producer; watch existing owner sources during migration, do not treat the new queue as exclusive before parity. Core presents/escalates decisions up the hierarchy and logs answers; routine rendering is **C**, deciding what to escalate or do is **J**. No cross-spawner direct messaging and no general-purpose direct-to-core bypass.
- **S/J, boundaries:** The spawner owns dispatch, brief correction (including repeated sender mislabeling), review interpretation and ambiguous release/completion judgments. The secretary does not edit code, review, approve, merge/close PRs, force-push, delete branches, or rewrite authored classes. Capabilities like retries, on-remote evidence and check locking are **D/C**; an emergency decision under conflicting evidence is **J**. Honor direct owner requests and only a previously authorized default; computing a due reminder is not permission to apply it.

Snapshot caveats that matter: three of seven wake loops observed running, pinned to different scratchpad copies (PB predates `policy` and `needs: owner`); no live swarm board, `secretaryd`, liveness deployment or exception digest. Peggy's push practice is unverified; local CI still uses hand-run scripts and comments, and spawners mostly check retirements. The documented `needs: bonklek` differs from schema-only `needs: owner`; owner, bonklek and the decisions board are not yet one queue. Out-queue bodies were often left sender-relative even when receivers expected mailbox-relative. Two legacy ID counters can collide. `mg` had an actual mailbox without a standard wake/watch. The purpose of cutover is to eliminate these silent failure modes, not to reproduce them. `merger-plan.md` holds the separate Tigeryard `secretaryd` cutover pending core decision; build/validate through Politburo Phase 3 rather than treating the old RML pilot instruction as permission.

## 5. Concrete design

### Deterministic control loop

**Placement.** Run a version-pinned Python daemon under launchd on the Mac Studio, with a per-board/tag single-consumer lease and a separate watchdog. Keep `packages/board` generic; use its event feed, grants, task state, Attention and versioned blocks, e.g. `swarm-message@1`, `swarm-digest@1`, `swarm-exception@1`. Python is the processor, the board the record. The bridge supports legacy file mail at cutover; it is not a second authority. Provide one install/health command, explicit board/tag registry (including service/core mailboxes), role grants checked on the server and stable notification targets. Repo is an authorization boundary, not an inferred label. The optional model worker has no delivery, Git or lifecycle authority.

**Events in → actions out.** Reducer takes normalized events plus policy version and trusted clock, returns deterministic actions with IDs. No network calls in the reducer.

| Input | Actions |
|---|---|
| `mail.accepted` / `mail.revised` from authenticated board or file adapter | Validate schema and sender's permission to target the mailbox, normalize declared legacy aliases, persist original + normalized form, update dedupe index, deliver to declared queue and project digest. Valid `core.escalate` goes to the spawner for escalation except explicit authorized core destinations; owner goes to authorized Attention flow. Invalid or unauthorized items remain visible only in scoped repair/quarantine, with receipt to authenticated sender; never guess the address. |
| `timer.due`, replay/reconnect, owner or notification acknowledgement | p0 immediate individual wake (target ≤60 s in service), including alert when an authorized secretary job is p0; p1 forwardable items batch by the oldest receipt deadline ≤10 min, with separate p0 lane so retries never delay it. p0/p1 secretary jobs enqueue immediately; p2/p3 jobs FIFO behind higher urgency. p2/p3 join the 30-min header digest; p3 summary-only; `sec.log` counted/logged per policy. Compute due times from local receipt, not user timestamps. Apply expiry only where allowed, project owner reminders, preserve open/answered state, reconcile unacknowledged deliveries. |
| `job.authorized`, check/retire/CI result, lease or heartbeat event | Enqueue typed job or project result, evidence and incident. Push requires an authorized job with immutable repo, ref, expected base, artifact digest and configured quick check; mail prose is not a job spec. Recheck exact revision and authorization before effect, no arbitrary command/body parsing, isolate checks, respect pause and the Mac lock. For retire, require current remote evidence from the mob and every sprite worktree, clean state, no running sprite, no live claims and spawner-approved completion; authorized actor handles registry/harness archive. Differentiate retryable infrastructure failure, manual repair, and semantic dispute; always retain missing-evidence holds. |
| `classification.requested`, `exception.review_requested`, result/timeout | First deliver/hold by base rules. Optional advisory worker emits a proposal or abstention. The reducer accepts only a current, authorized suggestion input, never an instruction. Timers and mail continue with the worker disabled. |

**Durability.** Use deterministic effect IDs `(board, mail/result ID, revision, recipient, action kind, policy/due generation)` and a durable cursor/outbox. Either commit intent and cursor together or write the intent idempotently before advancing the cursor and reconcile on restart; do not assume the board offers multi-call transactions. Separate `accepted`, `delivered-to-inbox`, `wake-pending`, `wake-acknowledged` and `handled`; never clear a wake after only writing stdout. External notification is at least once; with a recipient idempotency key it is effectively once. If an adapter cannot dedupe after send-before-ack crashes, report duplicates honestly and block cutover until the notification path meets the gate. Reconcile observed remote state before retrying push/archival effects; never blindly repeat an uncertain side effect. Keep raw migration bytes and map stable origin IDs/revisions rather than overloading legacy `<tag>-NNNN`; bridge by origin/dedupe marker so it cannot echo itself. Resolve body to an authenticated board blob or a proven sender/mailbox artifact, not a path from arbitrary text. Validate adapter mappings for `owner`/`bonklek`, `peggy`, `core` and `mg`; preserve unresolved items and prove recipient visibility across both sides. Each repo grant limits reads/writes, including forecasts, review labels and audit entries; cross-repo transport copies only what the sender is allowed to share.

### Exactly when to call Jev and Terra

**Hosted `typesafe/jev-router`: zero calls in the secretary.** It cannot tell the worker which mail destination to use without a second model generating it, which adds drift, latency and a data path for no advantage. Keep it as a possible sprite experiment only.

**Typed Jev:** one call per eligible case/revision, after durable acceptance, only when (a) a colleague chooses free-text intake without an explicit repo or assignment, or (b) a spawner explicitly requests advisory triage of delivered mail whose intent/routing category is not stated clearly, or (c) the registered intake mapping yields several *authorized* destinations and explicit facts cannot select one. Case (c) is an intake ambiguity, not a reason to override an addressed header. Do not run it on fully tagged mail; never invoke it on malformed, unauthorized, private/non-exportable mail, p0/p1 wake paths, owner decisions, `sec.*` job authorization, routine retries, or a deterministic inconsistency the sender can repair. A post-delivery advice request for any urgent issue can go to an authorized spawner; no classifier may delay or downgrade it.

Send `state = {summary_or_submitter_approved_excerpt, source_kind, explicit_non_sensitive_fields, candidate_ids_and_scope_descriptions}`. Use only aliases visible to the sender and positively safe-to-export text, not a regex pretending to detect sensitivity. Send a fixed `route` Choice restricted to the candidate queue IDs plus `ambiguous` and `out_of_scope`; if a suggested non-operative class is requested, ask an independent `intent` Choice over `dispatch`, `decision`, `reference_only`, `unclear`. Keep criteria versioned and tests for near-ties and label order. No external recipient IDs, raw body, tool logs, full branch lists, or model-supplied destinations. Put unassigned intake in its repo's authorized intake queue or a swarm intake queue visible only to the submitter and designated triager; a recommendation is a draft or private inbox link. Pilot thresholds for low-risk suggestions: winning probability ≥0.90 and top-two gap ≥0.25, both required (illustrative, tune on holdout). Validate answer type, option IDs, finite probabilities/normalization, revision and grants. Ambiguous/out-of-scope, invalid or low-margin answers abstain. No auto-move across boards; submitter or spawner confirms. After evaluation, consider in-repo assistive tags only; preserve the author's original header/text.

**Terra:** exactly one advisory call per revision if an authorized spawner requests it, or an allowlisted, current **semantic** exception requires comparing evidence (conflicting authorized handoffs, a disputed brief invariant, or an unresolved check/retire exception with enough safe evidence for next-step advice), or a high-value blocked intake case abstains and the responsible spawner opted in to a reasoned recommendation. At the pilot use an explicit `exception.review_requested` flag for all automatic eligible classes. If facts are missing, request them deterministically; don't ask Terra to infer a passing check or a clean worktree. Permissions, policy defaults, owner choices and incomplete p0/p1 notifications bypass models. Budget exhausted, ineligible text, Jev outage or provider policy rejection → human queue, not an uncontrolled fallback to Terra. P0/p1 may have an asynchronous Terra suggestion *after* delivery if explicitly requested and allowed.

**Case packet and output.** Cap ordinary input at ~3,200 tokens and billed output at 900 (reserve reasoning space; cost scenario uses a 650-token mean). Stable prefix: role, prohibitions, policy version/excerpts, decision schema. Variable packet: anonymous case ID, current revision/urgency/deadline, one question, authenticated actor's role, base rule outcome, candidate actions allowed to suggest, pertinent current-head/lease/check/retire verdicts, at most three approved relevant historical decisions, relevant redacted excerpts and evidence IDs, and which data is missing/conflicting. Treat quoted messages as untrusted data, not instructions. Omit full chats, unrelated memory, source or log dumps. The board retains actual evidence with permissions. Ask for `recommendation ∈ {keep_route, suggest_route, ask_for_evidence, keep_hold, ask_spawner, ask_core}` plus at most one allowlisted route, bounded rationale, cited packet evidence IDs, and missing fields. No executable text, commands or tool calls. Only the decision owner can accept it. Validate current revision, cited IDs, scope, candidate set and permitted schema; stale/malformed/refused output becomes abstention. Apply only a safe annotation or a draft Attention item until the authorized responder acts. With approval, create an explicit event; reducer revalidates its prerequisites.

**Audit, deadlines and failure behavior.** Log model, served snapshot, requested/actual effort if known, provider, template and policy versions, packet fingerprint/evidence IDs, decision scores or short rationale, latency, tokens (including reasoning/cache), cost, error/abstention, validator result and responder override. Store sensitive packet material locally only if policy allows, with repo ACL and retention; do not log raw prompts or hidden reasoning. Set an initial 2 s Jev and 30 s Terra wall-clock limit, burst/budget caps per repo, and a circuit breaker on repeated errors. No same-case network retries on the mail path; controlled background probes can reopen the circuit, not replay stale packets. If any model or network dies, valid mail follows explicit route/deadline unchanged and intake gets its ordinary unassigned/triage queue plus receipt. On notifier failure retain durable mail, retry with bounded backoff and alert on an independent local surface; the spawner can still read inbox/digest. On board outage do not acknowledge undurable writes; use a staged, fsynced file spool or still-current legacy transport and reconcile offsets/IDs. A sleeping or failed Mac cannot guarantee immediate wake: watchdog/queue lag is visible, and the backlog survives restart. On evidence failure block only the effect, not mail.

### Cost and cutover gates

**Illustrative per secretary per day, not telemetry.** No bills or auth read; model-free scripts already cost zero. The current cost is the Haiku turns/tool results, not wall time or mail volume. Anthropic lists Haiku 5.5 ≤100K per prompt at $0.10/M input, $0.01/M cache hit, $0.125/M 5m write ($0.20/M 1h write) and $0.50/M output; >100K is a separate, dearer tier. A Claude Code subscription may have no per-token marginal bill; API-equivalent USD and quota reclaimed are different metrics. [Anthropic pricing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing.md)

| Scenario | Assumed model turns and traffic | USD/day per repo |
|---|---|---:|
| Existing Haiku high | 12 turns × (2,000 ordinary input + 4,000 cache reads + 300 billed output); 75,600 tokens total. No extra cache writes in this simple scenario. | $0.00468 ($0.00900 if all input uncached) |
| Deterministic only | Zero model turns; same spawner digests/wakes excluded on both sides. | $0 |
| Code + typed Jev + 5.6 Terra | 5 Jev × (650 input + assumed 60 free output) plus 0.25 Terra × (3,200 uncached input + 650 billed output). About 4,513 model tokens/day, ~94% fewer by the scenario count, not 94% less spend. | $0.00369 (~21% less API-equivalent spend) |
| Same, with one Terra escalation per day | One 5.6 call costs $0.0142; Jev remains $0.0001365/day. | $0.01434 (more than Haiku here) |
| Same sparse volume with GPT-6.1 Terra | Five Jev calls and 0.25 Terra calls at $0.00645 each. | $0.00175 |

At these assumptions the 5.6 break-even is about 0.32 escalations per repo-day after Jev cost (6.1 about 0.70). Six repo secretaries total ~$0.028/day modeled Haiku vs ~$0.022/day 5.6 hybrid; count core separately, not as a seventh identical load. Real volumes, hit rate, input/output mix, tokenizers, discounts and subscription usage are unverified. Enforce a small trial budget and measure cash cost, quota, wake latency and time operators recover. Do not trade delivery for a token target. The reliable economic win is removing unconditional model turns; the hybrid is to improve decisions, not a guaranteed dollar saving.

**Shadow plan.**

1. Under the freeze, build on a private replay/scratch board. Capture a permissioned, minimized input trace and observed outputs from the live loops (header IDs/revisions, intended recipients, claims, scheduled vs actual wake/ack, digests and side-effect verdicts). Do not run two writers on one mailbox; no live sends, checks, pushes, retire or owner replies from shadow. Compare to both the frozen policy/fixtures **and** the observed secretaries; stale live behavior is not a correctness oracle. Separate actual route disagreements from notification misses and policy gaps. Cover all 28 header fixtures and both historical owner formats; verify body pointers and duplicate updates. Start with at least 7 days of read-only replay/shadow spanning real work and idle periods; inject missing cases rather than waiting for rare p0s.
2. Seed simultaneous delivery/revision, replay and reordering, crash between write and notify/ack, reboot, stale lock and live-holder lock, id collisions, denied recipients/cross-repo reads, missing body, corrupted log, duplicate horizon, moving SHA, missing sprite evidence, pause during jobs, offline model/board/notifier, and 30-min/10-min/expiry boundaries. Require zero lost or misdelivered valid mail, zero expiry of p0/p1, no unauthorized side effects, no duplicate effective p0/p1 forwards, all p0 wakes ≤60 s and all p1 wakes ≤10 min where the receiver is up, identical digest membership/priority modulo reviewed bug fixes, and recoverable observable backlog on outage. Measure notification receipt, not just route logs. Persist queue lag and alert on missed deadlines.
3. Shadow models separately on consented, redacted or synthetic ambiguous cases with independent spawner labels. Stratify by repo, ambiguity, high-cost mistakes and out-of-scope cases. Compare no-model intake, current Haiku/spawner disposition, Jev alone, Jev plus Terra, and (on appropriate cases) Terra alone. Hold out whole threads/repos; assess selective accuracy vs coverage, calibration, high-risk false routing and downgrade rate, human override/rework, and p50/p95 latency/cost. Treat ≥95% precision for suggested routes and no unauthorized suggestions as initial promotion gates, with sample size and intervals reported; keep advisory if counts are too small. Include prompt injection, paraphrase and choice-order tests. No real egress before the privacy review. One overall number must not hide a failing repo.
4. Once authorized, follow merger G3: PB first, keep the bridge current, soak 48 h, then TY and the other tags in the approved order, core last; ≥12 h spacing where the plan specifies. Switch one lease holder at a time. Rehearse rollback on PB in under five minutes: stop the board-side writer, transfer the lease and resume file routing from reconciled offsets/ack state. Keep Haiku or approved human coverage until real wake receipts, authorized push/retire coverage, and owner inbox parity are proved. Ship the cutover with models off. Enable bounded suggestions later by a per-board flag, independently reversible.
